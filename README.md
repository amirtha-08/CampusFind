# CampusFind – Campus Lost & Found Portal

## Problem Statement
Students often lose personal belongings within college campuses, such as ID cards, books, water bottles, and other essential items. Finding these belongings becomes difficult due to the lack of a centralized platform to report lost and found items.

## Problem Description
CampusFind is a web-based Lost and Found platform designed to help students report and recover misplaced belongings within their college campus. Users can browse found items and submit reports about lost or found belongings through a simple interface.

## Objective
To make finding and recovering lost belongings easier, faster, and more organized.
